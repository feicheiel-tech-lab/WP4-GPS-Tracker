Privacy Policy — WP4 GPSTracker

Last updated: 20 August 2026

WP4 GPSTracker - TrackMe ("the App") is developed by Feicheiel Technologies for use by opt-in participants in the MovingIMPACT WP4 research study. This part of the research aims to investigate the capacity of solar power needed to serve a certain number of EV's in the study area looking at the distance traveled, traffic density, and nature of road. This policy statement explains what data the App collects, why, and how it is handled.

Who this App is for

This App is intended for use by consenting participants enrolled in the MovingIMPACT WP4 study both as testers and app users. It is not intended for use by the general public and is not directed at children.

Data we collect

  • Precise location (GPS) — collected continuously, including while the App is in the background or the screen is off, so the research team can record participant movement patterns for the study.
  • Device identifier — used to associate uploaded location points with the correct study participant device.
  • Login credentials (username/password issued by the research team) — used to authenticate the App with the study server.

The App does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or call history, and contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. All data collected by the app are purely ambiguous (cannot be used to trace whose data is collected) and confidential (it is not shared with any 3rd party user).

Background location — why we need it

The App's core purpose is continuous movement tracking for the research study, which requires collecting GPS location even when the App is closed or not actively in use. Background location access can be revoked at any time in your device's Settings → Apps → WP4 GPSTracker → Permissions, though doing so will stop location tracking while the App is not on screen.

How your data is used

Location points, timestamps, and accuracy readings collected by the App are transmitted securely (HTTPS, token-authenticated) to the MovingIMPACT WP4 research server and used solely for the purposes of the study — analysis of participant movement patterns. Data is not sold, and is not shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Data storage and retention

Location data is cached locally on your device before being uploaded, and is stored on the research server for the duration of the study. Participants may request deletion of their data by contacting the research team at the address below; local on-device data can be cleared by uninstalling the App.

Data security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the research server is protected by per-participant authentication tokens issued by the research team.

Permissions used by this App

  • Location (fine, coarse, background) — core study functionality: recording participant movement.
  • Notifications — to show the ongoing tracking status notification required for a foreground location service.
  • Internet / network state — to upload location data to the study server.
  • Boot completed — to resume tracking automatically after the device restarts, so a reboot doesn't create a gap in study data.
  • Exact alarms — to schedule a reliable nightly data sync with the study server.
  • Ignore battery optimizations — requested so the operating system does not stop location tracking to save battery.

Children's privacy

This App is not directed at children and is only distributed to enrolled study participants.
